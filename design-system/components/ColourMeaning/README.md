# ColourMeaning

Every colour in tacit means one thing; this card shows each one doing its job.

- `ground-top`, `ground-mid`, `ground-deep`: the lavender ground, always as the 160-degree gradient with the grain on top (class `tc-ground`).
- `glow-peach`: one warm glow per screen, placed behind the thing that matters (set `--glow-at` and `--glow-size` on the `tc-ground` element).
- `glyph` and `cap`: cream for everything drawn or written on the ground.
- `listen`: blue only for "the band is listening". No blue buttons, links or decoration.
- `lit`: orange only while a skill passes from one person to another.
- `paper`, `ink`, `accent`: cards and chips on the ground.

To confirm: orange for "passing" came with the network view and was not discussed by itself. It is under 3:1 against every ground colour and reads through hue and its soft glow, so it must be looked at on the projector, especially over the peach glow.
