# SkillCard

One skill on a phone: what it is, a one-minute explanation in the person's voice, a before and after, and what to do with it.

- It answers three questions from top to bottom. What is it: "from" plus the handwritten name, a plain title, one line on when to use it. See it work: the player, then a grey "before" line and a lavender "after" line with the key fact highlighted. What to do: one main button and one text link.
- The phone is 324 by 708 and the whole card fits on one screen with no scrolling.
- The notification above it is white with the listening-blue app mark.
- The consumer provides: the person's first name, the skill title, the "when to use" line, the two example lines and the two actions.
- The unit is always "skill".

To confirm: the plus-sign flower that grows inside the player (liked here, turned down for the network), and the small capital labels on the card.
