# DoDont

The short list of what to do and what not to do, each shown as a pair.

- Say "skill", never "move".
- Section headings are plain phrases in italic serif, not small capital labels or numbered markers.
- Titles and the word `tacit` are lower case.
- Blue only for listening, orange only for a skill passing. Neither is ever a button, a link or decoration.
- A person is a small ring with a handwritten name; a group is a word and a denser patch of dots. Not flowers or petals, not big overlapping circles, not tiles, not braided threads, no outline around a group.
- Handwriting only for a person: their name and their own words.
- Pixel type only for the wordmark. No retro game look anywhere else.
- A card that shows a skill always says what the skill is, shows it working, and offers one thing to do.
