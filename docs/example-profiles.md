## Advice, as the team described it on 14 Nov

- Advice is what one person tells another to do or try. A skill is how I do something myself.
- Two kinds: general advice, and product advice, which is actionable ("You can up or down, you can decide you want to buy or not").
- Product advice carries a brand mark.
- It shows in two places: "a dedicated space for the advice people give you", and "what does my network recommend me using?"

Open: is advice a second unit next to "skill" or a kind of skill; does it live with the giver or the receiver; does the giver say yes before it is kept.
